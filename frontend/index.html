How it works

  1. 01
    Enter your GitHub username

    The backend fetches your contribution data securely – your token never leaves the server.

  2. 02
    Stats are calculated server-side

    Streak, total contributions, weekly and monthly counts are computed in Python.

  3. 03
    Data flows to the ESP32

    Via a live WebSocket connection, the processed JSON is pushed to the XIAO ESP32-S3.

  4. 04
    Sound & light come alive

    The ESP32 drives 364 LEDs, an OLED display, and a MAX98357A audio amplifier to turn your streak into music.
